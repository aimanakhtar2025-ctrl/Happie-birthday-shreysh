Happy Birthday, Shryss

A little piece of my heart, written just for you. ❤️

Shryss, aaj aapka birthday hai, aur main chahti hoon ki aapko sirf birthday wish na karun, balki woh sab bataun jo mere dil mein aapke liye hai.

Aap meri life ka woh person rahe hain jiske saath maine bahut saari feelings, memories aur moments feel kiye hain. Aapki chhoti chhoti baatein, aapki smile, aapka way of talking aur aapki presence mere liye hamesha special rahi hai.

Main perfect nahi hoon. Maine bhi mistakes ki hain, kabhi zyada possessive hui hoon, kabhi overthink kiya hai, aur shayad kabhi aapko unnecessarily hurt bhi kiya ho. Un sab ke liye I am genuinely sorry.

Lekin ek cheez main hamesha chahti thi — ki aapke saath hote hue mujhe feel ho ki main important hoon. Ki meri feelings matter karti hain. Ki aapki life mein meri ek special jagah hai.

Aapko shayad meri kuch feelings samajhna difficult laga ho, lekin maine jo bhi feel kiya, woh dil se kiya. Aapke liye mera care, attachment aur love kabhi fake nahi tha.

"Some people become memories, and some become a part of your heart."

Aaj aapke birthday par meri bas ek wish hai — aap hamesha khush rahein. Aapko life mein woh sab mile jo aap deserve karte hain.

Aur chahe future mein hum kahaan bhi ho, main genuinely chahti hoon ki aap smile karte rahein.

Thank you for every beautiful moment, every conversation, every laugh and every memory that you gave me.
